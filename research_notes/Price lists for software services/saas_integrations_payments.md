# Pricing presentation for SaaS subscriptions, integration platforms/services, and payment integrations

Research date: 25 Nov 2026. Figures were read from the live pages on that date unless a source date is given. Third-party (aggregator or competitor-blog) figures are marked as such.

## SaaS sold by subscription: page structure, units, discounts, trials, setup fees, enterprise tier; restaurant/POS/QR-menu examples

### Takeaway
Restaurant SaaS pricing pages almost always show 3 or 4 named tiers: a $0 or entry tier, a "best value"/"popular" middle tier, an upper tier, and a "custom pricing / custom quote / contact sales" top tier. Prices are shown "per month" and usually "per location". Extra devices (KDS screens, kiosks, extra registers, waiter handhelds) are priced as separate per-device monthly add-ons. Annual billing discounts on the pages checked run from about 10% to 33% ("Save 15%", "Save 17%", "33% off"). POS vendors that also process payments show the card rate per tier next to the software price, and a lower software fee comes with a higher processing rate.

### Cited Findings

**Square for Restaurants (US): 4 tiers, per location, card rate shown per tier**
- Square Free: $0/mo per location; card-present 2.6% + 15¢, online 3.3% + 30¢. Includes item/menu management, pickup and local delivery, and a branded online ordering page. — [Square for Restaurants pricing](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing)
- Square Plus: $49/mo per location, labeled "Best value", "Try free for 30 days"; 2.5% + 15¢ in person, 2.9% + 30¢ online. Add-ons: KDS app $30/mo per device, Kiosk $99/mo per device, Kiosk Lite $50/mo per device. — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing)
- Square Premium: $149/mo per location ("The best of the best with 24/7 support"); 2.4% + 15¢ in person; KDS add-on drops to $20/mo per device. — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing)
- Square Pro: "Get custom pricing", for businesses processing "over $250,000 per year"; may include custom processing fees, hardware discounts, onboarding support and account management. — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing)
- Pattern: add-on prices go down on higher tiers (KDS $30 → $20), which rewards upgrading. No annual discount appeared in the fetched content. — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing)

**Toast (US): the official pricing page returned HTTP 403, so these figures come from secondary sources**
- Toast's own blog (title seen in search results) presents a $0/month Starter Kit, a "Point of Sale" plan "starting at $69/month", and custom "Build Your Own" pricing. — [Toast blog: Toast pricing](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/toast-pricing) (fetch blocked; summary from search results only)
- NerdWallet (2026 review) gives 2.49% + $0.15 in-person processing on the paid plan with hardware bought upfront, a Handheld Starter Kit at $449.10 (or $0 with a higher processing fee), and a two-year processing contract on starter kits. — [NerdWallet Toast review](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/reviews/toast-pos)
- A third-party source puts Starter Kit pay-as-you-go processing at about 3.09%–3.69% + $0.15. Unverified against Toast. — [Owner.com: Toast pricing](https://www.owner.com/blog/toast-pricing); [UpMenu: Toast pricing](https://www.upmenu.com/blog/toast-pricing)
- Pattern: Toast trades a $0 software fee for a higher card rate and/or hardware cost.

**Lightspeed Restaurant (US): 3 priced tiers + Enterprise**
- Starter $69 ("Simplify your daily operations"), Essential $189 ("Exceed guest expectations"), Premium $399 ("Connect all of your departments"), Enterprise "Custom quote". The fetched text did not state the billing period or unit for these prices. KDS is $30 per screen per month on all plans. — [Lightspeed Restaurant pricing](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/pricing/)
- Integrated payments are shown as "Default" on Starter and "Custom" on Essential/Premium. Enterprise includes "Unlimited launch and consultation services". Disclaimer: "Prices may vary depending on the nature of the business and the industry." — [Lightspeed](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/pricing/)

**Poster POS (CIS-origin café POS, USD page): 3 tiers, menu-size gate, 15-day trial**
- Mini $26/$29 (100 products and recipes), Business "Popular" $44/$49 (300), Pro $62/$69 (1,500). Two figures are shown per plan next to an "Annual payment" label, and the lower one appears to be the annual-billing price (my inference; the page does not say). — [Poster pricing](https://joinposter.com/en/pricing)
- The tier gate is menu size (number of products and recipes), and higher tiers add features: Business adds modifiers, promotions, delivery, payroll, mobile waiter app and KDS; Pro adds reservations and advanced access control. — [Poster](https://joinposter.com/en/pricing)

Custom pricing above 1,500 items or for chains. — [Poster](https://joinposter.com/en/pricing)

**MENU TIGER (QR-menu SaaS): freemium + 3 paid tiers, gated by orders/month and stores**
- Free $0 (75 orders/mo, 1 store); Regular $17 (500 orders, 2 stores); Advanced $46 (2,000 orders, up to 5 stores, adds KDS and promotions); Premium $119 (unlimited orders and stores, built-in POS, table management, white label, priority VIP support). Monthly USD prices. — [MENU TIGER pricing](https://www.menutiger.com/pricing)
- Yearly billing "save 15%". No credit card needed for Free. "No per-order commission." Add-ons: Cloud POS $30/mo, Printer Management $20/mo (both included in Premium), White Label Domain $29/mo. Customers can keep ordering past the plan's order limit under a grace allowance. — [MENU TIGER](https://www.menutiger.com/pricing)

- The guest pays a platform fee per order (venues can absorb it). On the legacy platform it is 2.2% of the order total. The newer guest fee scales in bands by cart value, and a 1.7% card processing fee has been removed. — [me&u help: platform fee](https://help.meandu.com/hc/en-us/articles/17561701058703-Understanding-the-me-u-platform-fee); [me&u help: guest platform fee](https://help.meandu.com/hc/en-us/articles/17381321287567-Introduction-to-guest-platform-fee)
- A 2021 Square AU post said there were no upfront costs or subscription fees for venues. This is old (2021). — [Square AU / Mr Yum integration](https://squareup.com/au/en/townsquare/mr-yum-integration-au)

**General SaaS pricing-page patterns (strategy sources)**
- CXL: "4 seems to be the most popular number of plans among SaaS companies." — [CXL: 10 principles of effective pricing pages](https://cxl.com/blog/pricing-page/)
- 2015 analysis of 250 SaaS pricing pages (old): average of about 3.5 packages, and only half highlighted a recommended option. — [Process Street](https://www.process.st/saas-pricing-pages/)
- FastSpring: there is no standard tier count among best-in-class SaaS companies. — [FastSpring: three-tier pricing](https://fastspring.com/blog/three-tier-pricing-strategy-for-saas-is-it-still-ideal/)
- OpenView 2023 pricing data: 23% of surveyed SaaS companies had usage-based subscription tiers, 18% a largely usage-based model, 17% had tested it, and 42% had no usage-based pricing. — [OpenView 2023 pricing data](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/2023-pricing-data/)
- OpenView 2021 product benchmarks: 67% of developer-tool companies had a free plan or free trial, against 49% of horizontal apps and 36% of vertical apps. Freemium gave 33% higher visitor-to-user conversion, while free trials gave about 2x free-to-paid conversion (older data, 2021–2023). — [OpenView 2023 SaaS benchmarks](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/2023-saas-benchmarks/); [TechCrunch on usage-based pricing (2023)](https://techcrunch.com/2023/02/02/usage-based-pricing-is-rising-but-not-replacing-other-models/)

### Inferences
- A small studio selling a restaurant SaaS can follow the common layout: Free/Starter → "Popular" mid tier → Pro → "Custom/Contact us", priced "per location per month", with per-device add-ons and a visible "save X% yearly" toggle (15–17% is the common figure here).
- Restaurant SaaS gates tiers by business size (locations, orders per month, menu items, devices) more often than by seats.
- Of the restaurant examples above, only Toast (the "Point of Sale" plan, per its own blog) used "Starting at" wording. Most pages show a bare price with "/mo" plus the unit.
- None of the restaurant pages checked showed a published setup/onboarding fee. Onboarding appears either as a feature of the top tier (Square Pro, Lightspeed Enterprise) or is bundled into hardware/processing contracts (Toast).

### Gaps
- toasttab.com/pricing returned 403, so Toast's numbers are from its blog title and third-party reviews.
- iiko price page (iiko.ru/prices) returned 404. iiko and r_keeper pricing were not obtained.
- Lightspeed's billing period and unit (per location or per register) were not stated in the fetched text.
- No current independent study on annual-discount levels was found. Vendor blogs cite 15–20%, but their methodology is unclear.

## Integration platforms (iPaaS) and agencies selling integration services: pricing units, tier gates, fee structures

### Takeaway
Self-serve iPaaS tools price on a usage unit: Zapier "tasks", Make "credits", n8n "executions". Each tier bundles a volume of that unit with feature gates (multi-step workflows, users, SSO, environments), and the top tier is "Contact sales". Enterprise iPaaS (Workato, Tray, Celigo) is mostly sales-led. Integration agencies use three models: hourly/time-and-materials, fixed price per integration (scaled by objects and one-way vs two-way), or a fixed setup fee plus a monthly "continuity"/maintenance fee. Maintenance is often quoted as 10–25% of build cost per year.

### Cited Findings

**Zapier (unit: task)**
- Free $0: 100 tasks/mo, two-step Zaps only, 15-min polling. Professional from $19.99/mo billed annually or $29.99 monthly (750 tasks), with multi-step Zaps, premium apps, webhooks and 2-min polling. Team from $69/mo annual or $103.50 monthly (2,000 tasks, 25 users, shared workspace, SAML SSO). Enterprise custom via Sales. — [Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)
- Volume ladder (Pro, annual): 2,000 tasks $49; 10,000 $129; 50,000 $289; 100,000 $489; 2M $3,389. Annual billing is "33% off". — [Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)
- Unit definition: a task is counted when Zapier successfully completes an action. Triggers, filters and Formatter do not count, and failed actions are not counted. — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing)
- Overage: with pay-per-task on, extra tasks cost 1.25× the base rate on annual plans and 2.5× on monthly plans, capped at 3× the subscribed tasks. — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing)

**Make (unit: credit, formerly operation)**
- Plans are Free (1,000 credits/mo), Core, Pro, Teams and Enterprise. Most actions use 1 credit; router and error-handler modules are not counted. Extra credits come in bundles of 1,000 or 10,000. Annual payment is cheaper than monthly. The fetched page showed no dollar amounts. — [Make pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing)
- Prices conflict across third parties: G2 lists Core $9, Pro $16, Teams $29/mo (updated Apr 2026), while Emergent gives Core $16 ($12 annual) and Teams $51 ($38 annual). Unverified. — [G2 Make pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/make-formerly-integromat/pricing); [Emergent: Make pricing](https://emergent.sh/learn/make-pricing); [Zapier blog: Make pricing](https://zapier.com/blog/make-com-pricing/)

**n8n (unit: execution = one full workflow run)**
- Starter €20/mo (2.5K executions), Pro €50/mo (10K), Business €667/mo (40K, self-hosted), all billed annually; annual "Save 17%"; Enterprise "Contact Sales". An execution is "a single run of your entire workflow", whatever the number of steps. — [n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/)
- Tier gates: shared projects, concurrency, SSO/LDAP, environments, Git version control, audit logs. The page's plan card and comparison table disagree on which tier includes SSO and queue mode. — [n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/)

**Enterprise iPaaS (third-party summaries; vendor pricing not public)**
- Workato prices an edition plus usage credits. One source cites Pro from $75/mo for 2,500 credits for post-Feb-2024 customers; typical enterprise spend estimates range from ~$10K to $75–150K/yr (unverified). — [Activepieces: Workato pricing](https://activepieces.com/blog/workato-pricing); [Workato pricing page](https://workato.com/Pricing)
- Tray (vendor blog) describes a platform plan plus Tasks, with no per-user or per-connection fees. — [Tray: Workato pricing](https://tray.ai/blog/workato-pricing/)
- Celigo: G2 lists editions starting at $0, with paid tiers on custom pricing. — [G2 Celigo pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/celigo/pricing)

**Agency / integration-service pricing**
- Integrate IQ (HubSpot integration firm; vendor content, updated Oct 23 2026): fixed setup from $3,450 (1 object, one-way) to $18,400 (4 objects, two-way). Two-way costs ~33% more than one-way. "Continuity" is $250/mo one-way or $500/mo two-way. Hourly work outside fixed scope is $200/h. Large consultancies charge $200–$400/h. DIY tools (Zapier/Make/n8n) cost ~$20–$600/mo. Enterprise iPaaS costs $25K–$75K+/yr plus $50K–$150K implementation. — [Integrate IQ: CRM integration cost](https://integrateiq.com/blogs/crm-integration-cost/)
- No Bounds Digital (HubSpot agency; market ranges, not its own prices; undated): custom API builds $1,500–$3,000 simple, $3,000–$6,000 intermediate, $6,000–$15,000+ complex. HubSpot↔Salesforce $3,000–$5,000 basic, $10,000+ advanced; HubSpot↔ERP $10,000–$25,000. Maintenance is "often 10–20% of the initial project cost". Middleware costs $100–$500/mo. — [No Bounds Digital: custom HubSpot integrations](https://noboundsdigital.com/blog/guide-to-custom-hubspot-integrations-pricing-options-and-best-practices)
- Other vendor blogs (seen in search results, not fetched): per-connector fees of ~$400–$800 per connector per month (NetSuite-focused vendor); custom CRM↔ERP connector ~$18K–$45K build + $300–$1,100/mo support; upkeep 15–26% of build cost per year. — [Folio3 NetSuite integration cost](https://netsuite.folio3.com/blog/how-much-does-netsuite-erp-integration-cost/); [Black Badger: ERP integration cost](https://blackbadger.biz/blog/why-your-erp-crm-and-project-tools-do-not-talk-to-each-other-and-what-integration-really-costs); [Adapters.io: data integration cost](https://adapters.io/blog/data-integration-cost)
- SyncMatters markets "Custom CRM & ERP Integration Services, 45+ Connectors" with a flat monthly account fee and unlimited connectors (from search snippet only). — [SyncMatters custom integrations](https://syncmatters.com/custom-integrations)

### Inferences
- For a studio price list, the clearest structure is (1) a one-time "integration setup" fee scaled by scope (one-way/two-way, number of objects or entities, number of systems), (2) an optional monthly "monitoring & support" fee, and (3) an hourly rate for out-of-scope work.
- The one-way/two-way distinction and an object/entity count are concrete scope units that clients can understand. Integrate IQ's ~33% premium for two-way is one data point.
- Client-paid iPaaS subscriptions (Zapier/Make/n8n) should be listed as "billed separately by the provider", since their usage units vary.

### Gaps
- Make's exact current prices could not be read from the official page.
- Workato, Tray and Celigo have no public price lists; figures are third-party estimates.
- No Armenian or CIS agency integration price lists were found.
- Agency figures come from US firms writing content marketing, so they are biased toward their own model and are not market surveys.

## Payment integrations: provider fee presentation and how agencies price gateway integration

### Takeaway
Payment providers show fees as "% + fixed fee per successful transaction", with surcharges for international cards and currency conversion, and say "no setup, monthly or hidden fees". Large merchants get "custom pricing" or Interchange++. CIS providers use "от X%" or "X% + Y% за чек" headlines, note that rates exclude VAT, and say the final rate is set individually by contract. Agencies and freelancers price gateway integration from small fixed gigs (a basic checkout) up to five-figure projects (subscriptions/billing). I found no source showing a standard per-gateway fixed fee.

### Cited Findings

**Stripe**
- 2.9% + 30¢ per successful domestic card transaction; +1.5% for international cards; +1% when currency conversion is needed; +0.5% for manually entered cards. — [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)
- Wording: "Stripe does not charge setup fees, monthly fees, or any other hidden fees like closure fees." Custom packages are available for high volume (volume discounts, IC+ pricing, country-specific rates). — [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)
- Other fees listed: disputes $15 each; Instant Payouts 1.5% (min 50¢); standard payouts free. — [Stripe pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)

**PayPal (US, page last updated Nov 4 2026)**
- PayPal Checkout 3.49% + fixed fee; standard credit/debit cards 2.99% + fixed fee; Pay with Venmo 3.49% + fixed fee; international commercial transactions +1.50%; USD fixed fee $0.49. A separate fixed-fee table lists amounts for other currencies. — [PayPal merchant fees](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/paypal-business-fees)
- Monthly fees appear only on optional services (e.g. Invoicing Subscription $14.99/mo, Payflow Pro $25/mo). — [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/paypal-business-fees)

**Adyen (Interchange++ model)**
- Fixed processing fee $0.13 per transaction + payment-method fee. Visa/Mastercard: $0.13 + Interchange++ + 0.60%. Amex: $0.13 + 3.95%. ACH: $0.13 + $0.27. iDEAL: $0.13 + €0.22. — [Adyen pricing](https://www.adyen.com/pricing)
- "No monthly, set-up, integration, or closure fees". A minimum invoice may apply by industry; listed fees are indicative, and custom pricing is available. — [Adyen pricing](https://www.adyen.com/pricing)

**YooKassa (Russia)**
- Format "2,8% + 1% за чек" for cards, ЮMoney, SberPay, Mir Pay, T-Pay and Alfa Pay. SBP is "Рассчитаем индивидуально". The headline says "от 0,4%". Rates exclude VAT. The reduced rates require the "Чеки от ЮKassa" receipt service and run from 28.01.2026 to 01.04.2027 for new clients. Above 5M ₽/month turnover, contact a manager. — [YooKassa fees](https://yookassa.ru/fees/)

**CloudPayments (Russia)**
- The tariffs section states that the commission depends on business sector and turnover, that rates are indicative ("приблизительные и носят информационный характер"), and that the final rate is set individually in the contract. The rate figures did not render in the fetched page. — [CloudPayments payments/tariffs](https://cloudpayments.ru/payments)

**Russian bank acquiring (format examples, seen in search results)**
- Tochka: headline "Интернет‑эквайринг от 1,5%", free connection. — [Tochka internet acquiring](https://tochka.com/rko/internet/acquiring/)
- Alfa-Bank (search summary): free connection; price depends on industry and turnover; from 1 Jan 2026 bank internet-acquiring commissions carry 22% VAT. — [Alfa-Bank online payment for site](https://alfabank.ru/sme/payservice/onlain-oplata-na-saite/)

**Agency/freelancer pricing for payment gateway integration**
- techconcepts.org (Apr 30 2026, single vendor blog, assumes $150/h): simple one-time checkout $5,000–$15,000 (3–5 dev days); production B2B subscription billing $25,000–$50,000 (up to $60K in its FAQ); enterprise billing $50,000–$150,000; each later pricing change $3,000–$10,000. — [techconcepts: Stripe payment integration cost](https://techconcepts.org/blog/stripe-payment-integration-cost)
- Marketplaces: Contra says Stripe experts start at $25/h; Fiverr gigs list basic Stripe integration at around $50–$100 with 1-day delivery; Upwork profiles show $25–$75/h. — [Contra: hire Stripe freelancers](https://contra.com/hire/stripe-freelancers); [Fiverr: hire Stripe experts](https://www.fiverr.com/hire/stripe); [Upwork: Stripe developers](https://www.upwork.com/hire/stripe-freelancers/)
- A French marketplace reports an average day rate of about €130 for Stripe integrators (its per-hour conversion looks inconsistent). — [Codeur.com Stripe integrator rates](https://www.codeur.com/integrateur/stripe/tarif)

### Inferences
- A studio price list can show payment integration as a fixed line item per gateway ("Payment gateway connection: Stripe / PayPal / local bank vPOS, from X"), with separate higher-priced lines for subscriptions/recurring billing and for marketplaces/split payments. This follows the scope tiers in the techconcepts source.
- Every provider separates its own fees from integration labor. A studio should state: "Payment provider commissions (e.g. X% + fixed fee per transaction) are charged by the provider and are not included."
- The CIS habit of quoting rates "excluding VAT" and the bank VAT change in Russia from 2026 suggest the studio should state its VAT status explicitly.

### Gaps
- No studio or agency page with a published fixed price per payment gateway was found (US or CIS). The search returned mostly bank tariff pages.
- CloudPayments numeric rates did not render.
- Armenian gateways (e.g. Idram, ArCa, bank vPOS) and local agency rates were not researched in this pass.

## Wording patterns: units, "starting from", what's included, disclaimers

### Takeaway
Pricing pages repeat a small set of phrases: unit suffixes ("/mo. per location", "per device", "per screen per month", "for 10k credits/mo"), billing toggles ("Save 15%", "billed annually"), tier labels ("Best value", "Popular", "Get custom pricing", "Custom quote", "Contact sales"), "Everything in [lower tier], plus…", and disclaimers ("Prices may vary…", "no setup, monthly or hidden fees", rates excluding VAT, "final rate set individually by contract").

### Cited Findings
- Unit suffixes: "$49/mo. per location", "$30/mo. per device" — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing); "$30 per screen per month" — [Lightspeed](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/pricing/); "Price for 10k credits/mo" — [Make pricing (search snippet)](https://make.com/en/pricing); "€20/mo, billed annually … 2.5K executions" — [n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/)
- "From $19.99/mo" with a task-volume dropdown and "33% off" for annual billing — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing). "Save 17%" toggle — [n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/). "Save 15%" yearly — [MENU TIGER](https://www.menutiger.com/pricing)
- "Starting at $69/month" — [Toast blog (via search)](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/toast-pricing)
- Highlight labels: "Best value" (Square Plus), "Popular" (Poster Business) — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing); [Poster](https://joinposter.com/en/pricing)
- Top-tier labels: "Get custom pricing" (Square Pro), "Custom quote" (Lightspeed), "Contact Sales" (n8n, Zapier) — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing); [Lightspeed](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/pricing/); [n8n](https://n8n.io/pricing/); [Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing)
- Inclusion phrasing: "Everything in Free, plus…" — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing). "Included free in Premium" for add-ons — [MENU TIGER](https://www.menutiger.com/pricing)
- Trial wording: "Try free for 30 days" — [Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/point-of-sale/restaurants/pricing); 15-day trial with Pro features — [Poster](https://joinposter.com/en/pricing); "no credit card" for Free — [MENU TIGER](https://www.menutiger.com/pricing)
- Disclaimers: "Prices may vary depending on the nature of the business and the industry." — [Lightspeed](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/restaurant/pricing/). "No hidden or additional payments", hardware not included — [Poster](https://joinposter.com/en/pricing). "Stripe does not charge setup fees, monthly fees, or any other hidden fees" — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/pricing). "No monthly, set-up, integration, or closure fees"; fees indicative — [Adyen](https://www.adyen.com/pricing). Rates excluding VAT; individual terms above a turnover threshold — [YooKassa](https://yookassa.ru/fees/). Rates "приблизительные и носят информационный характер", final rate set by contract — [CloudPayments](https://cloudpayments.ru/payments)
- Commission framing: "No per-order commission" — [MENU TIGER](https://www.menutiger.com/pricing). "Per successful transaction" — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/pricing). Failed actions not counted — [Zapier](https://zapier.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- A studio price list for these categories can reuse: "from X" for scope-dependent services; "per location / per month" for SaaS; "one-time setup + monthly support" for integrations; and a standing footnote: "Third-party subscriptions (iPaaS, hosting) and payment-provider commissions are billed separately by the provider. Prices exclude/include VAT [state which]. Final price is set after a scope review."

### Gaps
- No systematic study of disclaimer wording was found; the patterns above come from the individual pages cited.
