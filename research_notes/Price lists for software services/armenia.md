# How Armenian (Yerevan) web/software studios present price lists

Research date: 25 Nov 2026. Pages were fetched on that date unless marked otherwise. "Fetched" means the page was read in full. "Snippet" means the figure was seen only in a search-result summary and was not checked on the page; treat those as unverified. One site (novaweb.am) could not be reached from this session (blocked by the session's network allowlist), so its "from 49,000 AMD" figure is snippet-only.

## Which Armenian companies publish prices, and which say "price on request"?

### Takeaway
Small and mid-size Yerevan web studios and freelancers often publish prices. Most use "from X AMD" starting prices, and a few publish full tiered packages (G Design Group, iMarketLab, Makyan Systems). Larger software/app houses (Astudio at the top end, DiNeuron, Codeex, CodeLines, app studios on Clutch/GoodFirms/TechBehemoths) give only high "from" figures, an hourly band in a directory, or no price at all ("free consultation", "individual estimate"). The marketplace turn.am is the main place where Armenian providers list a "from" price for each service.

### Cited Findings
**Studios that publish a full price list (fetched):**
- **Makyan Systems** (makyan.com) publishes the most detailed public price list found. It covers site packages, extra-service unit prices, hourly rates, payment-gateway integration and monthly maintenance tiers, with separate resident (AMD) and non-resident (USD) prices — [Makyan prices](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/prices/); [Makyan maintenance](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/maintenance/)
- **G Design Group** (gdesigngroup.net) publishes three named packages with AMD prices and build times: Ստանդարտ, Պրեմիում, Էլ-կոմմերս — [G Design Group](https://www.gdesigngroup.net/service/kayqi-patrastum)
- **iMarketLab** (imarketlab.am) publishes a six-tier WordPress price table with a feature matrix. It is an old promo: «Առաջարկը գործում է մինչ 2021թ․ օգոստոսի 31-ը» (the footer reads © 2025), so treat the prices as 2021 data — [iMarketLab](https://imarketlab.am/promos/wordpress-kayqeri-patrastum/)
- **Touch Web Agency** (touchweb.am) gives "from" prices for each category (landing, business, e-commerce) and lists what the business tier includes — [Touch Web](https://www.touchweb.am/services/web-development)

**Studios that publish ranges or articles instead of a price list:**
- **WebStart** (webstart.am) publishes a 2026 article with USD ranges by site type and recommends an individual estimate («անհատական գնահատում»). It shows no AMD price list — [WebStart 2026 article](https://webstart.am/for-client/vorqan-arje-kayi-patrastume-hayastanum-2026/)

**Marketplace listings: turn.am "Web Development" category (fetched, page 1 of 2, 30 of 59 listings):**
- Of the 30 listings, 24 show a "from" price and 6 show no price (Lemmon Business ×2, Websites-studio, WebON, Sunny Team, CodeLines) — [turn.am web development](https://turn.am/en/category/web-development)
- No listing on that page used a "contract price" or "price on request" label. Providers without a price simply showed none — [turn.am](https://turn.am/en/category/web-development)

**App/software companies in directories:**
- Directory pages for Yerevan app developers show hourly bands, e.g. VOLO and SOFTSHARK at $30–70/h and Quality Wolves at $70–150/h on TechBehemoths. GoodFirms lists X-TECH at $25–49/hr and DigiNeat at $50–99/hr (snippet) — [TechBehemoths Yerevan](https://techbehemoths.com/top-companies/mobile-app-development/yerevan); [GoodFirms Armenia](https://www.goodfirms.co/directory/country/app-development/armenia)
- MobileAppDaily lists minimum project sizes for Armenian firms, from "$1,000+" (Cupcake Development) to "$50,000+" (WaveAccess) (snippet) — [MobileAppDaily](https://www.mobileappdaily.com/directory/mobile-app-development-companies/am)

### Inferences
- Publishing a full tariff is a small-studio habit in Yerevan. The more "enterprise" the company, the more likely it gives a single high "from" figure or nothing.
- Prices are almost always in AMD for local clients. USD appears for non-residents (Makyan) or in content-marketing articles (WebStart).

### Gaps
- list.am service listings, staff.am, and Facebook/Instagram price posts were not reached in this session's searches. Their pricing practice is unverified.
- Page 2 of the turn.am category was not read.
- Clutch Armenia pages were not fetched.

## Pricing models used

### Takeaway
Four models dominate. (1) "from X AMD" starting prices (most common). (2) Named fixed packages with feature matrices: 3 tiers (G Design Group) or 6 tiers (iMarketLab), named in Armenian rather than Start/Business/Pro. (3) A price list of unit prices for add-ons plus hourly rates (Makyan). (4) Monthly maintenance retainers in named tiers (Makyan: Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum). Free support for a period after launch is bundled into higher tiers as a selling point.

### Cited Findings
- **"From" pricing:** Touch Web: "Landing pages start at AMD 50,000", "Business websites start at AMD 200,000", e-commerce "Projects start at AMD 450,000" — [Touch Web](https://www.touchweb.am/services/web-development). Makyan: "Website development prices start at 290,000 AMD" — [Makyan home](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/home/)
- **Packages with Armenian names (G Design Group):** Ստանդարտ 80,000 դրամ / Պրեմիում 150,000 դրամ / Էլ-կոմմերս 250,000 դրամ — [G Design Group](https://www.gdesigngroup.net/service/kayqi-patrastum)
- **Six tiers (iMarketLab, 2021 promo):** Պարզ / Պարզ+ / Հարմար / Հարմար+ / Շահավետ / Շահավետ+ ("Simple / Suitable / Profitable"), each with a "+" upsell — [iMarketLab](https://imarketlab.am/promos/wordpress-kayqeri-patrastum/)
- **Hourly rates (Makyan):** web programming "from 25,000 AMD per hour", SEO consultancy "20,000 AMD per hour". There is also a minimum fee of 8,000 AMD for any work — [Makyan prices](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/prices/)
- **Per-feature prices (Makyan):** web form up to 15 fields 55,000 AMD (+1,000 AMD per extra field); calculator form 95,000 AMD (+1,500 AMD per field); registration system from 300,000 AMD; control system from 250,000 AMD; subscription system 25,000 AMD; each extra language +25% of the site price — [Makyan prices](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/prices/)
- **Monthly retainers (Makyan):** 0 / 30,000 / 70,000 / 120,000 / 220,000 AMD per month per site. Higher tiers include free small changes and a discount on further development — [Makyan maintenance](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/maintenance/)
- **Bundled free-support period:** iMarketLab: no support on Պարզ tiers, «6 ամիս» on Հարմար tiers, «12 ամիս» on Շահավետ tiers — [iMarketLab](https://imarketlab.am/promos/wordpress-kayqeri-patrastum/). Touch Web's business package includes "one year of support" — [Touch Web](https://www.touchweb.am/services/web-development)
- **Discount levers:** a portfolio discount ("40% discount for portfolio projects"; the 40,000 AMD price "applies to projects I can showcase in my portfolio") — [turn.am, Alyona Golz](https://turn.am/en/service/razrabotka-saitov-dlia-malogo-biznesa-zaiavki-onlain-zapis-i-boty/detail). A seasonal banner on WebStart: «Բոլոր ծառայությունները մինչև −50%», «Մինչև նոյեմբերի 30-ը» — [WebStart](https://webstart.am/for-client/vorqan-arje-kayi-patrastume-hayastanum-2026/)
- **Resident/non-resident split:** Makyan's single-language PHP site costs "from 290,000 AMD" for residents and "from 1,000 USD" for non-residents. The online store costs "from 750,000 AMD" for residents and "from 5,000 USD" for non-residents — [Makyan prices](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/prices/)
- No published "setup fee + monthly fee" (SaaS-style) model from an Armenian studio was found.

### Inferences
- The local pattern is a one-off build price plus an optional monthly retainer. The retainer is sold by discount-on-further-work and number of free small edits, not by hours.
- Non-resident USD prices are 2–7× the AMD resident price at Makyan, so pricing is set by the client's market, not by cost.

### Gaps
- No Armenian studio page was found with a SaaS subscription price list (setup + monthly) for a custom product.

## Concrete price ranges per category

### Takeaway
Simple sites and landing pages: 40,000–80,000 AMD at the low end (freelancers, template studios), up to 390,000 AMD (Astudio landing). Business/corporate sites: about 150,000–590,000 AMD. E-commerce: 250,000 AMD (template) to 750,000 AMD (Makyan) to 2,900,000 AMD (Astudio). Custom systems and integrations: "from $3,000". App/custom-software companies quote "from 10,000 USD" or $25–150/h. Maintenance: 30,000–220,000 AMD per month.

## Page wording and terms (languages, units, VAT, timelines, payment terms)

### Takeaway
Pages use «սկսած» / "from" / «от» and are mostly in Armenian with EN/RU versions. Package names are Armenian words, not English tier names. Timelines are given in days per package. VAT (ԱԱՀ) and prepayment terms are almost never stated; Makyan is the exception with "Prices include taxes" on the maintenance page.

### Cited Findings
- WebStart uses «…$-ից» and «սկսվում է 3000$-ից» and tells readers to get an individual estimate — [WebStart](https://webstart.am/for-client/vorqan-arje-kayi-patrastume-hayastanum-2026/)
- G Design Group writes prices as "80 000 դրամ" and timelines as «Կայքի պատրաստումը 5 օր» — [G Design Group](https://www.gdesigngroup.net/service/kayqi-patrastum)
- iMarketLab uses a feature matrix marking excluded items «չկա» (e.g., «Վճարային համակարգերի ներդնում» — "integration of payment systems" — is «չկա» in all six tiers). It limits the offer to Armenian residents and small businesses — [iMarketLab](https://imarketlab.am/promos/wordpress-kayqeri-patrastum/)
- turn.am shows "Service price from: … AMD" and "Service avg. time"; one listing states payment by "bank transfer, cash, and crypto" and "Signs a contract" — [turn.am, Alyona Golz](https://turn.am/en/service/razrabotka-saitov-dlia-malogo-biznesa-zaiavki-onlain-zapis-i-boty/detail)
- Makyan's maintenance page: "Prices include taxes" (no VAT rate named). Its prices page: "The exact price is determined following a free consultation"; complex design or extra functionality "can raise the price" — [Makyan maintenance](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/maintenance/); [Makyan prices](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/prices/)
- Touch Web, G Design Group, WebStart and iMarketLab: no VAT and no prepayment terms on the fetched pages — sources as above.
- Russian-language listings exist on turn.am (e.g., «Разработка сайтов для малого бизнеса», «Сайт для бизнеса с нуля под заявки») — [turn.am category](https://turn.am/en/category/web-development)

### Inferences
- A 50/50 prepayment is likely common practice but is not published on any page checked, so it cannot be cited.
- For a product page aimed at Armenian SMBs, stating whether a price includes ԱԱՀ would already stand out from the competition.

### Gaps
- No source found stating 50/50 or other prepayment percentages.
- No explicit «ԱԱՀ-ն ներառված է» / «без НДС» wording found on a studio page.

## Pricing of payment gateway integration and acquiring fees

### Takeaway
Only Makyan publishes an integration price: "from 200,000 AMD" for online payment via Ameriabank, Evocabank or IDBank. Payment providers publish onboarding steps but rarely merchant fees. Card acquiring through a bank vPOS costs roughly 1.5% (ArCa cards) to 2.7% (Visa/MC). Stripe is not available to Armenian-registered companies.

### Cited Findings
- Makyan: "Online payment integration (Ameria Bank, Evokabank, IDbank): from 200,000 AMD" — [Makyan prices](https://www.makyan.com/en/page/prices/)
- G Design Group bundles «Առցանց վճարում (Visa / Master)» into the 250,000 AMD e-commerce package with no separate fee — [G Design Group](https://www.gdesigngroup.net/service/kayqi-patrastum)
- iMarketLab: payment-system integration excluded from all WordPress tiers — [iMarketLab](https://imarketlab.am/promos/wordpress-kayqeri-patrastum/)
- Vexagency (Nov 2026): "Stripe does not open accounts for businesses registered in Armenia". It quotes ACBA vPOS fees of 1.5% for ArCa cards, 1.8–2.7% for local Visa/MC, 2.3–2.7% for foreign cards and 3.0–4.0% for Amex, with setup "10–15 days after the application is accepted". Idram contacts applicants within 5 working days and connects within a few days of signing; Telcell issues credentials on contact; no wallet fees are stated. Aggregators bundle cards + Idram + Telcell (+ ArCa QR) for an extra fee. These are second-hand figures from an agency blog — [Vexagency](https://vexagency.co/en/blog/stripe-alternatives-armenia)
- Bank requirements reported by the same source: AEB requires HTTPS and an Armenia-registered domain; Ameriabank requires prices in AMD; banks check SSL, company details, terms, refund/privacy/delivery policies and an Armenian-language version — [Vexagency](https://vexagency.co/en/blog/stripe-alternatives-armenia)
- AEB's vPOS page: the bank gives test credentials generated by ArCa ("Armenian Card"); live credentials follow after integration is confirmed; the site needs a domain, real IP and HTTPS (snippet; per-transaction fee not extracted) — [AEB vPOS](https://www.aeb.am/en/vpos)
- IDBank promotion: no internet-acquiring fees for 3 months after terminal activation, then the bank "will propose further fees" (snippet) — [IDBank news](https://idbank.am/en/information/about/news/news/idbank-s-new-offer-to-the-business/)
- Byblos Bank Armenia's tariff lists 1.75% for ArCa cards and 2.2% for foreign Mastercard/Visa. It is unclear whether this applies to vPOS (snippet, unverified) — [Byblos POS](https://www.byblosbankarmenia.am/en/terminals/POS-terminal)
- Free WordPress/WooCommerce plugins exist for ArCa (incl. Idram support), which lowers integration effort for template stores — [ArCa payment gateway plugin](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/wp/plugins/arca-payment-gateway/trunk/readme.txt)

### Inferences
- In this market a payment integration is a sellable add-on worth on the order of 200,000 AMD, or a bundled line in a store package.
- Because Stripe is unavailable, integrations with local banks and wallets are a real differentiator for Armenian products.

### Gaps
- No current published merchant commission found for Idram, Telcell or Easypay online payments.
- No primary bank tariff page (Ameriabank, Inecobank, ACBA) was fetched; the ACBA percentages come from a secondary source.
- No studio price found specifically for Idram, Telcell, Easypay or PayPal integration.
- novaweb.am (snippet: "from 49,000 AMD") could not be fetched because the host is not on this session's network allowlist.
