# How international software/web agencies present price lists for custom products (US/UK/EU + Eastern European outsourcing)

Research date: 25 Nov 2026. Sources are mostly 2025–2026. Most figures come from vendor marketing pages (list prices, not market averages). Survey/directory data (Clutch, Accelerance, Techreviewer) is flagged separately. Where a fact came only from a search-engine summary and the page was not opened, it is marked "(search summary)".

## 1. Which pricing models dominate on agency pricing pages?

### Takeaway
Most custom-dev agencies do not publish a full price list. They publish "starts at $X" / "from X" anchors plus a range, push a paid or free discovery step, and quote fixed price per milestone after scoping. Published fixed tiers (Small/Medium/Large, Starter/Growth/Enterprise) show up mainly for narrow, repeatable scopes: discovery, UX/UI design, store setup, audits, maintenance/support. Dedicated-team and support offers are priced per month. Hourly rates usually appear only for post-launch or ongoing work.

### Cited Findings
**"Starts at" + range + discovery + milestone fixed price (most common pattern for custom builds)**
- RaftLabs (MVP page): "Starts at $10K"; "A typical MVP build runs $25,000 to $60,000"; "Projects start at $10K, with scope and price agreed in writing before the build"; first milestone priced "at a fixed cost agreed before development starts"; no packages or tiers — [RaftLabs MVP](https://www.raftlabs.com/services/minimum-viable-product-mvp-development)
- Presta (Belgrade, Serbia) publishes a table of "starting prices" per engagement type. Its FAQ says the prices help clients self-qualify and are not a menu: "Final scope and cost depend on complexity, integrations, and timeline: every quote is custom." Every engagement begins with a Discovery Workshop and ends in "a fixed-price proposal with a week-by-week timeline" — [Presta pricing](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)
- GetDevDone (San Francisco) mixes fixed productized items (Website Tune-Up $399, website build $499 first page + $149/page) with an "Advanced Website Build: On brief (defined after discovery)" tier and a "Get a Quote" CTA — [GetDevDone order page](https://getdevdone.com/order-now/website-development)

**Paid discovery as a separate, tiered product**
- Geniusee (US entity, delivery hubs in Kyiv/Lviv, Ukraine and Warsaw, Poland) lists the Discovery Phase as Small $5,000 / Medium $10,000 / Large $15,000 "total". The tiers are sized by the expected project (3 months / 6+ months / 12+ months with a 5-FTE team) and take 2/4/6 weeks — [Geniusee Clutch packages](https://clutch.co/profile/geniusee/mobile-app-dev-package)
- Presta: Discovery Workshop "From 2,500 EUR", credited to the build — [Presta pricing](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)
- First Pier (Shopify): paid diagnostic $1,950 and migration assessment $2,450. "Every dollar paid for a diagnostic or assessment credits toward any project or retainer signed within 90 days" — [First Pier packages](https://www.firstpier.com/packages)

**Dedicated team / monthly team pricing**
- Geniusee "Software Engineering Services" are priced per month: Small $30,000/mo, Medium $60,000/mo, Large $90,000/mo. Tiers differ by team composition: back-end and front-end/mobile full-time in all tiers; DevOps, QA and PM part-time vs full-time; designer and tech writer "upon client request and will be estimated additionally" — [Geniusee Clutch packages](https://clutch.co/profile/geniusee/mobile-app-dev-package)
- Clutch's software pricing guide lists the pricing models as hourly, fixed-price, time & materials and dedicated team, but gives no rates per model — [Clutch software dev pricing](https://clutch.co/developers/pricing)

**Subscription / productized dev ("unlimited requests")**
- Byteex: Build $2,500/month ("Unlimited requests, one active at a time"), Changes $999/month, Maintenance $499/month, Hosting $29/month. Month-to-month; "pause or cancel at the end of any month" — [Byteex custom software](https://byteex.co/services/custom-software)
- Other subscription dev services found: Rapid Native $1,999/month tier, at most two tasks in progress; Reframe Labs offers unlimited requests worked one at a time, with pause up to 2 months (search summary) — [Rapid Native](https://www.rapidnative.com/pro); [Reframe pricing](https://reframe.shadcn.io/pricing)

**Hourly**
- Presta: 50 EUR/h regional, 70 EUR/h international, and "Ongoing development after launch: 70 EUR per hour", hourly work "invoiced monthly for hours used" — [Presta pricing](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)

### Inferences
- For a small-café product company, the agency norm to copy is: a few fixed, narrow packages with fixed price and fixed timeline, plus "from" anchors for anything custom, plus a paid or free discovery step whose fee is credited to the build.
- Fully published Starter/Growth/Enterprise tiers for whole custom apps are rare among credible agencies. When they exist (Geniusee), they are monthly team budgets with screen and feature caps, not fixed project prices.

### Gaps
- No quantitative survey found on how often each model (fixed vs T&M vs dedicated) appears on agency pricing pages. Techreviewer's 2025 study did not cover pricing models ([Techreviewer 2025](https://techreviewer.co/research/software-development-companies-rates-in-2025)).

## 2. Typical published ranges per category (with region) and what packages include

### Takeaway
Directory data puts most listed agencies at $25–$49/h, with typical projects at $10k–$50k. Averages are much higher because of large projects: about $66k for web and $91k for mobile on Clutch. In Eastern Europe, published starting prices are roughly €5k–10k for an MVP or company site at a Serbian studio, and $10k+ at US/global studios. Retainers range from about $500/month (maintenance only) to $1.5k–6k/month with 5–34 hours included. Bot pricing is mostly from freelancers ($30–$300 start), and the agency data is thin.

### Inferences
- Even "cheap region" agencies anchor custom builds at €5k+ (Serbia) or $10k+ (global). Small fixed products (store setup, tune-ups, bots) are priced at $300–$4,000.
- Retainers are sold by included hours plus response SLA, with minimum terms of 3–6 months, or as flat-fee tiers named by outcome ("Keep it running", "Changes").
- Clutch's typical ranges (median-like) and averages differ by 5–10×. When citing "average cost", note that a few large projects skew the average.

### Gaps
- No published Georgia or Armenia agency rate cards found. No country rates for Serbia or Georgia in the Clutch or Accelerance public pages; the Accelerance full guide is gated.
- UK rates show as "Unknown" on Clutch pages. No credible UK-specific 2025–26 survey found.
- Agency (not freelance) Telegram/WhatsApp bot price lists with full tier contents were not found on credible agency sites; data is mostly from marketplaces.
- GoodFirms 2026 website survey (300+ firms, 31 countries; $1,000 basic to $150,000+ enterprise) could not be opened (HTTP 403); figures from a search summary only — [GoodFirms website cost survey](https://www.goodfirms.co/resources/website-construction-cost-survey?b60bd69c_page=1)

## 3. Wording patterns: section names, units, disclaimers, CTAs, payment terms

### Takeaway
Pages use plain, reassuring wording: "Starts at", "from", "flat", "fixed price, no surprises", "every quote is custom", "The scope is the scope, the price is the price". The unit is always explicit: "one-time", "/month", "total", "per hour", "per 60 minutes". Payment terms are milestone or phase-based, usually with a deposit, and are rarely published in detail. CTAs are low-commitment: "Book a call", "Get a Quote", "Tell us about your project", "Start Your Scope".

### Cited Findings
- Units and labels:
  - "Starts at $10K" — [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/services/minimum-viable-product-mvp-development)
  - "From 5,000 EUR", "Credited to the build", "Per 60 minutes" (expert call 150 EUR), "Per hour" — [Presta](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)
  - "$1,950 flat" — [First Pier](https://www.firstpier.com/packages)
  - "fixed price (up to 1 day of work)", "$499 first page, + $149 / page after" — [GetDevDone](https://getdevdone.com/order-now/website-development)
  - "$X total" vs "$X/month" — [Geniusee](https://clutch.co/profile/geniusee/mobile-app-dev-package)
  - "one-time" vs "/month" — [ECOSIRE](https://ecosire.com/services/shopify/pricing)
- Disclaimers:
  - "Final scope and cost depend on complexity, integrations, and timeline: every quote is custom." — [Presta](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)
  - "The scope is the scope, the price is the price." "There are no discounts on these packages, and no hourly upsells inside them." "A limited number are taken each month." — [First Pier](https://www.firstpier.com/packages)
  - Changes are priced and approved before the work; ongoing hosting and third-party costs are excluded from the build price — [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/services/minimum-viable-product-mvp-development)
  - "Shopify subscriptions are not included and are billed separately" — [ECOSIRE](https://ecosire.com/services/shopify/pricing)
  - "Third-party usage costs, such as AI model tokens or paid APIs, are billed at cost on top" — [Byteex](https://byteex.co/services/custom-software)
  - Explicit "Excludes" lists per package (e.g. e-commerce, custom plugins, design/content); additional work "requires a quote" — [GetDevDone](https://getdevdone.com/order-now/website-development)
  - Items outside a package marked "upon client request and will be estimated additionally" — [Geniusee](https://clutch.co/profile/geniusee/mobile-app-dev-package)
- Payment terms:
  - "Pay by milestone. You see progress before each invoice." IP transfers "with each milestone payment." — [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/services/minimum-viable-product-mvp-development)
  - A deposit secures the start date; remaining payments are "tied to delivered phases, not the calendar"; hourly work is invoiced monthly for hours used; all terms appear in the proposal — [Presta](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)
  - The ASAP start needs a refundable deposit, with a full refund if the date is missed — [GetDevDone](https://getdevdone.com/order-now/website-development)
  - Month-to-month, "move up or down at the start of any month", source-code buyout "available on request" — [Byteex](https://byteex.co/services/custom-software)
  - Retainers carry minimum terms of 3–6 months — [Freelock](https://www.freelock.com/product/freelock-retainer-packages)
- CTAs:
  - "Start Your Scope", "Get a straight answer"; free 30-minute call "whether or not you build with us" — [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/services/minimum-viable-product-mvp-development)
  - "Tell us about your project" — [Presta](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)
  - "Order Now" for fixed items and "Get a Quote" for custom ones — [GetDevDone](https://getdevdone.com/order-now/website-development)
  - "Book this package", "Prefer to talk? Book a call", plus a free lead magnet: "Drop your Shopify store URL and we'll send back 3 quick wins — free, no pitch" — [First Pier](https://www.firstpier.com/packages)
  - "Start building" / "Book a call" — [Byteex](https://byteex.co/services/custom-software)
  - "Get Started", "Start Growth Plan", "Book Free Consultation", "Need a custom package? Let's talk." — [ECOSIRE](https://ecosire.com/services/shopify/pricing)
  - Each Clutch package tier shows a "Request a Quote" button — [Geniusee](https://clutch.co/profile/geniusee/mobile-app-dev-package)
- Trust phrases: "you own 100% of the code" — [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/services/minimum-viable-product-mvp-development); "Full ownership of code, design files, and accounts" — [Presta](https://wearepresta.com/pricing?view=full)

### Inferences
- A common two-CTA split: a direct order/book button for fixed packages, and quote/call for custom work.
- Several pages (GetDevDone, First Pier, Byteex) list what is excluded alongside what is included, which pre-empts scope disputes.

### Gaps
- No source found that publishes a specific upfront percentage (e.g. 30/40/30 or 50/50) on a pricing page. Pages say "deposit" or "milestone" without percentages.
- Tax/VAT wording ("excl. VAT") was not seen on the pages reviewed.

## 4. Good examples of transparent price pages and credible rate surveys

### Takeaway
The most transparent agency examples found are Presta (Serbia; starting-price table, inclusions, payment terms, hourly rate), Geniusee's Clutch packages (UA/PL; tiered discovery, design, team, mobile and support with hours and screens), GetDevDone (per-page pricing plus add-ons), First Pier (flat fixed-scope sprints with fee credit), Byteex (subscription ladder) and Freelock (hours-based retainers). For rates, Clutch's live pricing guides and Accelerance's annual guide are the most-cited sources. Both have methodological limits.

### Inferences
- For Pativ-style pricing, Presta's single-page model is the closest template for a small Eastern European studio: a starting-price table, a "what's included in every engagement" list, payment terms in plain words, and an hourly rate for after launch.
- Directory and survey rates disagree with each other. For example, Clutch puts the US at $25–$49 on the mobile page but $100–$149 on the web page. Bands should be quoted with their source and treated as indicative.

### Gaps
- Upwork rate reports were not reviewed (time budget).
- The Accelerance country tables (Poland, Ukraine, Serbia, Georgia) are behind a download form and were not accessed.
