## 1. Who we are

Pativ («Պատիվ») is a service of [company name], TIN [TIN], address [address] ("Pativ", "we"). You are the café or restaurant that signs up ("the café", "you"). The person who signs up confirms that they may act for the café.

## 2. What Pativ is

Pativ is a web service for cafés. It gives you:

- a QR menu for guests in Armenian, Russian and English;
- a menu import from a photo, which you review before publishing;
- on the Service plan, table ordering from the guest's phone, kitchen and bar screens, a waiter app, Telegram and phone alerts, and a sales dashboard.

Nothing needs installing. Staff open the service in a browser.

## 4. Invoices and payment

- There is no card payment in Pativ. We send an invoice in AMD each month (or once a year for the annual plan), and you pay by bank transfer or at the bank, to [bank account details].
- The invoice is due within [number] days. If it is more than 7 days late, ordering turns off and the location falls back to the Menu plan, as in section 3. Nothing is deleted.
- Invoices come from [company name]. Tax documents for our fees follow Armenian rules [accountant to confirm].

## 5. What Pativ does not do

- **Pativ does not issue fiscal receipts (HDM).** Keep your cash register and print your receipts exactly as you do now. A bill shown in Pativ is not a fiscal document.
- **Pativ does not process payments.** Guests do not pay in the app. Staff record that a table paid in cash or by card at your own terminal, and the money never passes through us.
- Pativ is not an accounting, tax or stock system.

## 6. Your responsibilities

You are the publisher of your menu. That means:

- **Menu accuracy.** Names, descriptions, prices and photos must be correct and up to date.
- **Allergens and food information.** You decide what to tell guests about allergens and ingredients, and you stay responsible for it. Pativ does not check it.
- **AI import and translations.** The photo import and the Russian and English translations are written by an AI service and can be wrong. Nothing is published until you have reviewed it. Check every price and every dish name, and have someone who knows the language read the translations. By publishing, you accept the text as your own.
- **Your accounts and staff.** Keep passwords private, remove staff and devices that no longer work for you, and make sure staff use the service properly.
- **Guest data.** You deal with your guests. How Pativ handles personal data is in the [Privacy notice](privacy.md).
- **Lawful use.** Don't upload content you have no right to use, don't try to break or overload the service, and don't use it for anything unlawful.

## 7. Pilot period

During the pilot the service is new. It is provided "as is":

- we aim for it to run on every opening day, but we do not promise a minimum uptime and we do not guarantee that it is free of errors or interruptions;
- we answer support requests through the Telegram support chat; in the pilot we aim to give a first answer within 15 minutes during opening hours if the service is stopped, the same day for something important that is broken, and the next working day for questions [hours and channels to confirm];
- the staff screens keep working for a short time without internet and catch up afterwards, but you should keep a paper fallback (pad and pen) at the bar;
- we may change or remove a feature. We tell you about important changes in advance.

## 8. Your data and export

- The menu, prices, photos and sales records you enter belong to you.
- You can export your menu, orders and bills to a spreadsheet (CSV) from the admin at any time, also after the free month and also on the Menu plan.
- We use your data only to run the service for you, as described in the [Privacy notice](privacy.md). We do not sell it.
