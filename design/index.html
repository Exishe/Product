Four screens of a Yerevan café, clickable

Design direction A, “Tarragon and apricot”. You can add dishes, send an order, start cooking, close a table and review a menu the AI read from a photo. The café and its data are fictional.

Screens

  • Guest phoneMenu, order and live status Switch Հայ, Рус, Eng. Search “khorovats” in any script. Send the order and watch it move to “Ready”.
  • Kitchen tablet, 1280 × 800Kitchen board Paper-slip tickets readable from 2 metres. A new order arrives 6 seconds after you start the shift; a cancellation needs a tap.
  • Waiter tablet, 1180 × 820Floor and table Accept a QR order, mark food served, answer a call, close table 5 with cash, card or both.
  • Owner laptop, 1440 × 900Menu from a photo Check what the AI read from the paper menu. Publishing stays locked until every dish is checked.

Tablet and laptop screens shrink to fit a phone. Turn the phone sideways to see them bigger.

The three directions

A. Tarragon and apricot

Colours from the table: tarragon, apricot, pomegranate. A menu that reads like a printed café menu. Recommended, and used in these mockups.

B. Basalt and brass

Evening Yerevan, dark and warm. Good on a laptop at night, weak on a terrace at noon. Kept for the kitchen board only.

C. Vernissage

Flat colour blocks and rug motifs from the Vernissage market. Memorable, tiring on a busy shift. Kept for empty states and onboarding art.
